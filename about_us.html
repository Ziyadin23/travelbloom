About Us

TravelBloom is a fictional travel discovery company created for a JavaScript course project. Its purpose is simple: help curious travelers compare a small selection of beaches, temples, and countries.

We value clear descriptions, cultural respect, and considered travel. These recommendations are starting points for research; check official visitor information before planning a real trip. This example company does not sell or arrange travel.

Our team

Team guidance

Before publishing a recommendation, check its factual description, confirm the image credit and license, and test both the search and its keyboard controls. Keep sample content clearly identified and collect no unnecessary personal information.
